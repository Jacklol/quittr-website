Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 22, 2026

Application: Quittr

Developer / Service Provider: Ina App LTD

Contact: allienjayproject@gmail.com

Ina App LTD ("Service Provider," "we," "us") offers the Quittr mobile application ("Application") as a Freemium service.This Privacy Policy details how we handle, process, and protect data collected when you install and use Quittr.

1. Information Collection & Usage

A. Data Provided Directly by You

While basic functionality of Quittr does not require mandatory account registration, certain features may be restricted without it. If you choose to register or interact with support, we may process the credentials or details you provide. We may also use your contact details to communicate essential service updates, legal notices, or marketing materials.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you open and navigate Quittr, our systems automatically collect non-sensitive technical identifiers to optimize performance, including:

  • Device hardware model and unique device identifiers (e.g., IDFA/GAID);
  • IP address and approximate geographical region;
  • Mobile operating system and version;
  • Network browser types and internal application usage analytics.

2. Location Tracking

Quittr does not collect, monitor, or track precise real-time GPS location data from your device.

3. Third-Party Integrations & Service Providers

To monitor app stability, analyze product analytics, and deliver custom features, Quittr relies on external service platforms. Only anonymized or aggregated data is shared with these third parties to support operations.

External SDKs and Partners:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — App usage & telemetry analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Crash reporting & performance monitoring
  • Adapty — In-app purchases & subscription handling

iOS Specific: Meta (Facebook) SDK

On iOS devices, we incorporate the Meta SDK to evaluate user engagement and evaluate marketing campaign efficiency.Meta may collect event logs (such as app activations or purchases), advertising IDs (IDFA, where permitted), and IP-derived broad location data. You can manage or revoke tracking permissions at any time via your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Android Specific: AccessibilityService API

The Android version of Quittr utilizes the AccessibilityService API strictly to enable core screen-time management and digital mindfulness functionality.

  • Purpose: Detects when specified applications are launched to present a pre-access reflection screen or mindful pause.
  • Guarantees: Quittr does not read, log, store, or transmit screen text, personal information, or keystrokes. It does not perform automated actions on your behalf. All processing occurs strictly on-device. Accessibility access can be revoked via Android System Settings at any time.

4. Disclosure Obligations

We reserve the right to disclose processed data only under specific circumstances:

  • When mandated by applicable law, court order, or formal legal process;
  • When necessary to investigate potential fraud, enforce terms, or safeguard user safety;
  • With vetted service providers who act strictly on our behalf and have agreed to adhere to these privacy standards.

5. Opt-Out Rights & Data Retention

  • Opt-Out: You can cease all data collection by removing the Application from your device using standard uninstall procedures.
  • Retention: Automatically gathered telemetry is retained for up to 24 months, after which it is anonymized into aggregate statistics. User-provided data is retained for the duration of your application use.
  • Data Deletion Request: To request full deletion of your user-provided information, please contact us at allienjayproject@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Quittr is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not intentionally gather personal information from minors. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately delete it.

7. Security Standards & Policy Revisions

We maintain administrative and technical procedures designed to shield your data against unauthorized access. However,no digital transmission or storage technology can guaranteed 100% security.

We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be published directly on this page. Continued use of Quittr after modifications are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
