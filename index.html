Meet Quittr ↘

A quieter space on your iPhone.

Small moments. Meaningful choices.

A little space.
A better next choice.

You don’t need to change everything at once.
Start with a pause, and take it from there.

01

Pause. Breathe. Choose.

Press and hold. Take 30 seconds to slow down, breathe, and come back to the present.

02

See your days.

Check in on your calendar. Mark a day of self-control or acknowledge a setback. Every day is a new choice.

03

Keep going, gently.

Collect achievements as your days add up. A difficult day doesn’t erase the progress you’ve made.

Your progress stays with you.

Your answers, calendar and achievements are stored on your iPhone.
